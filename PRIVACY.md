# viewsnap Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-26

viewsnap does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data.

## What the extension does
When you press the viewsnap keyboard shortcut, the extension captures the visible part of the active tab as a PNG image and saves it to a folder inside your Downloads directory. The image is written directly to your disk. It is never uploaded anywhere.

## Data handling
- **No data collection.** viewsnap gathers no browsing history, page content, analytics, telemetry or identifiers.
- **No network requests.** The extension does not contact any server, including the developer's.
- **Local settings only.** Your subfolder name and filename template are saved with `chrome.storage.local` on your device. They are not synced and nobody else can read them.
- **Page URL and title.** The page's hostname and title are read only when you press the shortcut, and only to fill in the `%host` and `%title` placeholders in the filename. They are not stored or sent anywhere.

## Permissions
- `activeTab`: captures the current tab, and only after you press the shortcut.
- `downloads`: saves the screenshot to your Downloads folder.
- `storage`: keeps your settings on your device.

## Third parties
viewsnap shares no data with third parties, because it has none to share.
